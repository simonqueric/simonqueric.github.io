About

I'm Simon Queric, a 1st year PhD candidate under the supervision of Charles Bouveyron and Marco Corneli in the Inria Maasai team. My main research interests include statistical machine learning, optimal transport and analysis of graph data. I am also an engineer graduated from Télécom Paris and I hold a Msc in Machine Learning (Maths, Vision, Learning) from ENS Paris-Saclay.

My PhD is funded by the 3IA Côte d'Azur institute.
